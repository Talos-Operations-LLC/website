Built to be checked.

Nobody should take software at its word on who owns a property. Every answer in Fee Simple can be traced back to the record it came from.

Matched by parcel number

The tax card and the recorded deed are tied together by the county's parcel number, not by a name or a street address.

Says when records disagree

The tax card can still show the previous owner after a new deed is recorded. When that happens, you get a finding, not a guess.

Every source opens

Each finding opens the recorded document or the county page it came from.

Clear about what it is

A report summarizes the public records Fee Simple read. It is not a title opinion or a commitment to insure.

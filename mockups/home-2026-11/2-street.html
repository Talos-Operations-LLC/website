How it works, in three panels.

The county keeps two records of who owns a property. They don't always agree. Fee Simple reads both.

1

It reads the tax card.

That's the county tax office's record: who gets the bill, and where it's mailed.

2

It reads the deed.

That's the Register of Deeds' record: who the property was actually deeded to.

3

It matches them up.

Both records are matched by parcel number. Then you get one plain answer.

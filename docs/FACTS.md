# Facts ledger

Source notes supporting the Learn and audience pages, restored under `public/`.
These notes were carried forward from the original site, not reverified during
the restructure. Reverify sources when changing or extending claims.

**Pending rows block merge.** A branch carrying a page whose claim has a `pending` row in the "verified by" column does not merge to `main` until that row is verified, or the claim is reworded or removed.

Every factual claim on a public page gets a row here, in the same change as the page. Definitions and process descriptions (what a deed is, what a grantor is) do not get rows; claims about how counties, offices or records actually behave do. No client names, addresses, or book/page references in this file.

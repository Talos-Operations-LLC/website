How do you find the owner behind an LLC-owned property in NC?

When a deed names an LLC, the county records stop at the entity. The NC Secretary of State's business registry lists the LLC's registered agent and its filing history, and some records list company officials such as a manager. Matching the deed's grantee name to the registry filing is how people find who is behind an entity-owned parcel.

What happens when a deed names an entity?

When property is held in an LLC, the deed names the LLC as the grantee and the tax card lists the LLC as the owner. Neither record shows the people behind it.

How do you search the NC Secretary of State's registry?

  1. Copy the entity name exactly as the deed shows it, punctuation included.
  2. Search the Secretary of State's business registry by that name.
  3. Open the matching record. It shows the legal name, SOSID, status, date formed, registered agent, registered office address and registered mailing address.
  4. Check for a "Company officials" section, which can list a manager or other official with a name and address.
  5. Use "View Filings" to see the filing history and open filed documents, such as the Articles of Organization.

Registered agent, member, manager: what does each listing mean?

  • Registered agent: the person or company the LLC designated to receive official papers on its behalf. It can be an individual or a commercial registered-agent service.
  • Member: an owner of the LLC.
  • Manager: a person who manages the LLC, who may or may not also be a member.

How do you join the parcel to the filing?

An exact name match is the starting point. Comparing the addresses on the registry record with the tax card's mailing address can help tell similar names apart.

Where does the trail stop?

  • Out-of-state LLCs. An LLC formed in another state may be registered in North Carolina as a foreign LLC. Its application for a certificate of authority names the jurisdiction where it was formed, which is where the search goes next.
  • Layered LLCs. The listed member or manager is itself another entity, and the search repeats one level up.
  • New LLCs. A newly formed LLC may not list any company officials yet, so the record shows only the registered agent.
  • Formation services. When an LLC is formed through a registered-agent or formation service, the Articles of Organization can list only that service as organizer and registered agent, so no owner's name appears in the filings.

Frequently asked questions

Is the registered agent the owner?

Not necessarily. The registered agent is whoever the LLC designated to receive official papers. It can be an individual, who may or may not be an owner, or a commercial registered-agent service. The listing alone doesn't say who owns the LLC.

Are Secretary of State records free?

Yes. Viewing business records on the NC Secretary of State site is free. The fees printed on Secretary of State forms are for filing documents, not for viewing them.
